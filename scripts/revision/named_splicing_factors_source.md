# The named splicing factors used in Figs. 2, 3 and 8

One list is used in every figure, and it is a published panel rather than a
selection of our own: the twenty splicing regulatory factors assayed in senescing
primary human dermal fibroblasts by Holly et al. 2013 (Mech. Ageing Dev. 134,
356-366; PMID 23747814), Table 3. All twenty are reported there as age-associated
in that cell type: seventeen lower in late-passage cells and three higher
(HNRNPUL2, PNISR, SF3B1) - the direction must be stated, not smoothed over.

`named_splicing_factors.txt` is that list, verbatim from Table 3.

Highlighting (Fig. 2b): only four of these are reported as age-associated in more
than one of the studies cited by this manuscript, all of them falling - SRSF1,
SRSF2, SRSF6 and HNRNPK. That is the only criterion under which the phrase
"repeatedly reported" is defensible.

Genes previously labelled "named in the ageing literature" in our figures for
which no human age association exists in any cited paper: U2AF1, SNRPA, SNRPB,
SNRPD2, SNRPF, SF3B5, RBM39, PRPF19, PRPF31, DDX39B, CELF1, MBNL1; PRPF3 and
PRPF8 (mouse only); SF1 (C. elegans only); SF3A1 and SF3A3 (measured, not
significant); ESRP1 and PTBP1 (human, but reported up).
